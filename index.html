FAQ

Do I need surfing experience?

No. Our lessons are suitable for complete beginners as well as intermediate surfers.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear and a towel. Surfboard, rash guard, reef shoes, sunscreen and zinc are included.

Where do the lessons take place?

We surf in Nusa Dua, Kuta, Balangan, Jimbaran and Uluwatu, depending on conditions and your surf level.

How do I book a lesson?

Simply contact one of our instructors via WhatsApp and arrange your lesson directly.
